# Topic bank

True stories with a clear "it backfired" or "nobody expected this" moment, easy to show with people, animals and places. Rotate eras, countries and times of day so episodes look different. Check every fact again before writing.

1. **The Great Emu War (1932)** - Australia sent soldiers with machine guns against emus. The emus won. (Example episode: `episodes/emu_war`.)
2. **Boston Molasses Flood (1919)** - a giant tank of syrup burst and a sticky wave rolled through the streets.
3. **London Beer Flood (1814)** - a brewery vat burst and a wave of beer flooded the neighbourhood.
4. **Tacoma Narrows Bridge (1940)** - "Galloping Gertie" twisted in a steady wind until it tore itself apart.
5. **Citicorp Center (1978)** - a student's question revealed a skyscraper could fall; it was fixed secretly at night.
6. **The Great Stink (1858)** - London's river smelled so bad Parliament finally paid for sewers built twice too big, still working today.
7. **Abraham Wald and the bullet holes (WWII)** - armour the parts of returning planes WITHOUT holes.
8. **Semmelweis (1847)** - a doctor told other doctors to wash their hands and was laughed out of Vienna.
9. **De Havilland Comet (1954)** - why airplane windows are round.
10. **The exploding whale (Oregon, 1970)** - highway workers removed a dead whale with dynamite.
11. **Reversing the Chicago River (1900)** - engineers made a river flow backwards.
12. **Mars Climate Orbiter (1999)** - a 125-million-dollar spacecraft lost because one team used inches.
13. **The Gimli Glider (1983)** - a jet ran out of fuel mid-air because of a units mix-up and glided to a racetrack.
14. **The Vasa (1628)** - Sweden's mightiest warship sank 1,300 metres into its first voyage.
15. **Hubble's blurry mirror (1990)** - a billion-dollar telescope was off by 1/50 of a hair; astronauts fitted it with "glasses".
16. **Hanoi rat bounty (1902)** - paying for rat tails created rat farms.
17. **Ten Cent Beer Night (1974)** - a baseball promotion that ended the game early.
18. **The Dancing Plague (1518)** - hundreds of people in Strasbourg danced for days and could not stop.
19. **Tulip mania (1637)** - one flower bulb cost as much as a house, then nothing.
20. **Leaning Tower of Pisa** - it started leaning before it was finished, and that is why it never fell.
21. **Operation Acoustic Kitty (1960s)** - spies tried to turn a cat into a listening device.
22. **The Kentucky Meat Shower (1876)** - chunks of meat fell from a clear sky.
23. **The Pig War (1859)** - the US and Britain nearly went to war over a pig; the only casualty was the pig.
24. **The Toledo War (1835)** - Ohio and Michigan fought over a strip of land; the "loser" got the Upper Peninsula, which turned out to be rich.
25. **Victor Lustig sells the Eiffel Tower (1925)** - a con man sold it for scrap. Then tried again.
26. **Rabbits in Australia (1859)** - 24 rabbits released for hunting became hundreds of millions; a fence across a continent followed.
27. **Lake Peigneur (1980)** - an oil drill punched into a salt mine and a whole lake drained into it.
28. **Millennium Bridge (2000)** - London's new bridge wobbled on opening day because people walked in step.
29. **New Coke (1985)** - Coca-Cola changed its recipe and had to bring the old one back in 79 days.
30. **The Eiffel Tower was supposed to be temporary (1909)** - it was saved because it made a great radio antenna.
